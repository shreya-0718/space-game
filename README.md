# Voyage to the Stars

Hello! Welcome to this collaborative Godot game that me and my friends made- it's called *Voyage to the Stars*.
It follows the story of a space explorer from a different planet who happens to be passing by our solar system. But- oh no! The crystal that powers the rocket shatters into many shards. Each of these shards lands on a different planet. Now, we must help our character face the perils of the planets to return home safely. 

This game was built in Godot with me and three of my friends! All of the art is custom art made by us! The music is non-copyrighted music from Pixabay.

Overall, making *Voyage to the Stars* was a huge bonding experience for all four of us. We're improving upon it more (with more levels and side quests!), so stay tuned!

Play it HERE: [https://ntexas201201.itch.io/voyage-to-the-stars](https://ntexas201201.itch.io/voyage-to-the-stars)
